# Document Operations Agent

**Pack:** `document-operations`  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Tier:** A  
**Readiness:** Pilot base  
**Operating mode:** approval-gated

## Purpose

Turns incoming PDFs, scans, email attachments and office files into validated records and routed exceptions.

## Buyer

Accounting, logistics, wholesale, property management, agencies and document-heavy back offices.

## Outcome

A measurable straight-through document pipeline with field-level evidence and human review where needed.

## Promise

> From messy document to verified record without losing the source.
